\subsection{Solver implications of the proved matrix structure}
\label{sec:solver-implications}

The raw matrices are nonsymmetric and invertible. We use a sparse direct
baseline through SciPy's \texttt{spsolve} interface~\cite{scipyspsolve},
with CSC input and \texttt{use\_umfpack=False}, explicitly fixing the
SuperLU backend. Each call includes a fresh factorisation; no factor is
reused between timed repetitions.
Multiplication by the invertible positive $W_h^c$ gives the equivalent
system $K_h^c\boldsymbol U_h^c=W_h^c\boldsymbol b_h^c$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:energy} proves the symmetry and positive
definiteness required for CG~\cite{scipycg}. Its diagonal is positive
because $(K_h^c)_{kk}=\boldsymbol e_k^TK_h^c\boldsymbol e_k>0$.
Consequently, the Jacobi inverse
$\operatorname{diag}(K_h^c)^{-1}$ is an admissible positive definite
preconditioner. The baseline and cross-checks below test these solver
choices; the structural proof alone makes no runtime claim.
